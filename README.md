## Disciplus

Mathematics requires constant practice, but manual checking and identical printed sheets take up too much time and effort for teachers. Services like “YaClass” offer automated checking, but they are not adapted to the Estonian curriculum. At the same time, students at the target school are already used to Opiq and YaClass, so they will accept the new tool without any problems. And building the system using open‑source technologies such as Moodle, Numbas, and H5P will make it possible to use the developments beyond the scope of a single project. 
How to build a convenient platform based on an open‑source LMS with task generation and gamification that will meet the needs of grades 4–6 under the second stage of the Estonian curriculum?
Objective is To design, develop, and test a web platform with varied, automatically graded math assignments for grades 4–6, as well as to assess its impact on student engagement and performance.
